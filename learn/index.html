Glossary

AI agent payment
A payment initiated by software acting on behalf of a person or business, such as an AI assistant paying a supplier invoice.
Mandate
A signed instruction from the account holder defining what an agent may do: payees, limits, accounts, expiry and exception rules.
Know Your Agent (KYA)
Verification of an agent's identity, operator and the party it represents.
Evidence pack
One record per agent action showing identity signal, mandate version, checks, approver and the instruction sent to the payment rail.
Security procedure (UCC 4A)
A procedure agreed between bank and business customer to verify payment orders; if commercially reasonable and followed in good faith, the customer is generally bound.
MCP
Model Context Protocol, a common way for AI applications to connect to tools and services.
Personal Agent Protocol
A draft open standard from Sierra and Meta (October 2026) for how personal agents sign in to businesses and request scopes.
AP2
Google's Agent Payments Protocol, which uses signed mandates for agent-initiated commerce.
