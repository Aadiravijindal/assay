      01 · The problem
      Agents are at the door. Today's options all fall short.
      
        
      Business owners want AI agents to pay suppliers, top up payroll and move cash. Today banks either let agents log in with the owner's password, block them, or allow read-only access that stops short of payments.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      OPTION A
      Shared passwords
      The agent can do anything the owner can. You can't tell them apart, and nothing records who decided what.
      
        
      OPTION B
      Blanket blocks
      Safe this quarter. Then the customer, and their deposits, move to whoever lets their agent in.
      
        
      OPTION C
      Read-only access
      Useful for balances and insight. It stops at the step businesses actually want help with: paying.

      04 · Architecture
      Fits between the agent and your core.
      
        
      No new rails and no custody. Assay uses existing agent-identity signals, implements the agent protocols on your side and hands approved instructions to the systems you already run.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      
      
      
      
        
      Uses, doesn't rebuild, agent identity.Assay records which provider's signal was used for each decision.
      
        
      Bank limits always win.You set ceilings by account type. The customer's mandate works inside them.
      
        
      Sold with your vendors.We expect to deploy with and through digital-banking and core providers.
      
      
      
    
      
  
      

  
  
      
    
      
      
      
        
      05 · Why now
      In October 2026 the biggest banks said "not like this."
      
        
      Nobody has said how. The standards for agent access don't yet cover mandates, approvals, evidence or liability.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      13 OCT 2026
      "Rogue agents"
      JPMorgan's CEO says the bank won't let just any outside agent in, per press reports.1
      
        
      14 OCT 2026
      A person in the loop
      Bank of America's CEO says a person should approve money leaving an account.1
      
        
      MID-OCT 2026
      Read-only
      Several of the largest US banks restrict outside agents to read-only access.1
      
        
      STILL OPEN
      No standard
      The Personal Agent Protocol draft lists payments and signed approvals as open work.4 5
      
      
      
      
      Why business accounts first
      Under UCC 4A, a business customer is bound by a payment order when the bank followed an agreed, commercially reasonable security procedure in good faith. Assay's mandate, approval and evidence are built to be that procedure for agent payments. Your counsel decides.3
      
    
      
  
      

  
  
      
    
      
      
      06 · Questions
      From risk and compliance teams.
      
        
      Assay is a prototype. We don't yet hold a SOC 2 report, and we won't claim customers or partners we don't have.
      
      
      
        
      Does Assay hold or move money?
      No. Assay decides whether an agent's request may proceed and records what happened. Approved instructions go to your existing payment rails.
      
        
      Is this live?
      Not yet. Assay is a prototype and we can show you a live demo. We are recruiting design partners before any pilot.
      
        
      Do we replace our agent-identity or fraud tools?
      No. Assay uses identity signals from existing agent-verification providers and sits alongside your fraud and payment controls.
      
        
      Who sets the limits, the bank or the customer?
      Both. You set ceilings by account type. The customer signs a mandate within them. Neither can exceed what the other allows.
      
        
      How fast is the off switch?
      Immediate for new requests. One agent, one customer's agents or all agents. Customers can switch off their own.
      
        
      What about consumer accounts?
      Not first. Consumer liability for agent payments under Reg E is less settled. We start where the rules are clearest.
      
        
      How would we buy it?
      We expect to work with and through your digital-banking and core providers. If you're one of them, talk to us.
      
        
      What does it cost?
      We'll set pricing with our design partners.

      → Security · Oct 2026
      
  
      Security and principles.
      
  
      What Assay is designed to do, what it will never do, and what we don't have yet.
      
  
      Never
      • Hold, pool or move customer money.
      • Ask a customer to share their banking password with an agent.
      • Let an agent act outside a signed mandate without a human approval.
      
  
      How decisions are made
      • Refused unless a signed mandate allows it or a person approves it.
      • Mandates are signed with a passkey and expire. Approvals use passkeys.
      • Your own limits and controls still apply.
      
  
      Evidence
      Per action: agent identity signal, mandate version, checks run, approver and time, the instruction sent to your rails, and a hash so the record can be checked later.
      
  
      Standards
      Designed for MCP, the Personal Agent Protocol draft and AP2. Where they don't yet cover mandates, approvals or evidence, we intend to contribute rather than fork.
      
  
      Not yet
      No SOC 2 report or other certification. No production deployments. Data-handling details are covered in the demo and will be published here before any pilot.
